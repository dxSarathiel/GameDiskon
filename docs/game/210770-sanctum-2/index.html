Tentang Sanctum 2

Sanctum 2 adalah game aksi, indie dan strategi buatan Coffee Stain Studios, diterbitkan oleh Coffee Stain Publishing, dirilis 15 Mei 2013. Fitur yang tercantum di Steam antara lain pemain tunggal, multipemain, co-op dan co-op online.

Deskripsi resmi di halaman Steam:

Sanctum 2 is the sequel to the world’s first Tower Defense/FPS hybrid game. Pick from four unique character classes and embark on a mission to protect the oxygen-producing Cores from hordes of deadly aliens who are threatened by their existence.
