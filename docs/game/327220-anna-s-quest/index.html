Tentang Anna's Quest

Anna's Quest adalah game petualangan dan indie buatan Daedalic Entertainment, dirilis 2 Jul 2015. Fitur yang tercantum di Steam antara lain pemain tunggal, pencapaian Steam, dukungan kontroler penuh dan trading card Steam.

Deskripsi resmi di halaman Steam:

With her telekinesis, unorthodox use of torture instruments, a knack for improvisation and the help of a shady fox she makes her way from the peaks of Glass Mountain to the deepest dungeon.
