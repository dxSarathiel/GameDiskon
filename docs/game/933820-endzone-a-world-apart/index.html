Tentang Endzone - A World Apart

Endzone - A World Apart adalah game indie, simulasi dan strategi buatan Gentlymad Studios, diterbitkan oleh Assemble Entertainment dan WhisperGames, dirilis 18 Mar 2021. Fitur yang tercantum di Steam antara lain pemain tunggal, pencapaian Steam, trading card Steam dan takarir tersedia.

Deskripsi resmi di halaman Steam:

Endzone is a post-apocalyptic survival city builder, where you start a new civilization with a group of people after an ecological disaster. Build them a new home and ensure their survival in a shattered world, threatened by constant radiation, toxic rain, sandstorms and droughts.
