Tentang Arcade Spirits

Arcade Spirits adalah game indie buatan Fiction Factory Games, diterbitkan oleh PQube, dirilis 12 Feb 2019. Fitur yang tercantum di Steam antara lain pemain tunggal, pencapaian Steam, dukungan kontroler penuh dan kontrol volume kustom.

Deskripsi resmi di halaman Steam:

Arcade Spirits is a romantic visual novel that follows an alternative timeline where the 1983 video game crash never occurred. It's 20XX and you've just started a new job at the Funplex game arcade. Who will you meet along the way? Will you find the romance you're seeking?
