Tentang The Land Beneath Us

The Land Beneath Us adalah game indie dan strategi buatan FairPlay Studios, diterbitkan oleh Dear Villagers dan Gamirror Games, dirilis 13 Mei 2024. Fitur yang tercantum di Steam antara lain pemain tunggal, pencapaian Steam, dukungan kontroler penuh dan dukungan kontroler dualshock.

Deskripsi resmi di halaman Steam:

A fast-paced, turn-based rogue-lite dungeon crawler. Unleash skill combos, wield a variety of weapons and battle through the mythic Underworld of Annwn. Conquer the 7 Lords, collect Soul-Tech, and save The Creator. With real-time tactical decisions and endless replayability, runs are never the same!
